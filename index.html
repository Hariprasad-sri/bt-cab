Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our billing, booking confirmations, and driver safety standards.

In a Single Trip (One-Way Drop Taxi), you only pay for the distance travelled from your pickup point to your destination. You DO NOT pay for the car’s return journey! In a Round Trip, the cab stays with you throughout your journey for sightseeing and return travel.
Our taxi fare covers the vehicle rental, fuel charges, and driver bata. Fast-tag toll charges, hill station parking fees, and interstate permits are actuals paid directly by the passenger at the toll booths.
